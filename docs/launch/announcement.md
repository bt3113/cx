# Announcement copy

## Launch post

**Zat — everything that makes you, you, in one place.**

We built Zat because the most useful recommendations on the internet are also
the most disposable. Someone you trust tells you what camera they actually use,
and 24 hours later it is gone.

A Zat is a personal world you own. Spaces are the areas of your life. Items are
the things inside them — and every Item carries the context that makes it worth
trusting: how it was obtained, how long it has been used, how often, what is
wrong with it, and what to consider instead.

Commerce is present and deliberately quiet. Disclosure is structural, not a
footnote: an Item cannot exist on Zat without stating its commercial
relationship, and that label travels with it everywhere — including onto the
Story image you share.

Identity first. Exploration second. Trust third. Commerce fourth.

Free to start.

## Product Hunt

**Zat — A creator-owned personal world, not a link list**

Instagram shows what you posted. Linktree shows where you exist. Storefronts
show what someone wants you to buy. Zat shows the person.

Spaces are areas of your life. Items are the things inside them, each with
disclosure, duration of use, frequency, likes, dislikes, alternatives and the
content they appear in. Every profile, Space and Item generates a 1080 × 1920
Story image with the disclosure rendered into it.

Built for people whose taste is part of their work — and for anyone who is
tired of recommendations they cannot weigh.
