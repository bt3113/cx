# Positioning

## One line

Everything that makes you, you — in one place.

## Short description (50 words)

Zat is a creator-owned personal world. One link holds your identity, taste,
tools, work and recommendations, each with the context that makes it worth
trusting: how you got it, how long you have used it, and what is wrong with it.
Not a link list. Not a storefront.

## Long description (150 words)

Instagram shows what you posted. LinkedIn shows what you do for work. Linktree
shows where you exist. Affiliate storefronts show what someone wants you to buy.
None of them show the person.

Zat is a persistent digital world you own. Spaces are the areas of your life —
Wardrobe, Camera Bag, Books, Current Rotation. Items are the things inside them,
each carrying the evidence that makes a recommendation worth believing:
disclosure, duration of use, frequency, what works, what does not, and the
content it actually appears in.

Commerce exists, deliberately last. A visitor should finish exploring your Zat
understanding you better than when they arrived — and a recommendation they can
weigh properly is worth more than one they have to guess about.

One link. Your identity, your taste, your work, your world.

## Creator pitch

You answer the same question every week. What camera, what jacket, what chair.
The answer lives in a story for 24 hours, or in a comment nobody finds again.

Zat turns that answer into something permanent. Write it once, with your real
reasoning attached — including what you dislike about the thing. It keeps
answering for years, it is measurable, and it belongs to you rather than to a
feed.

Publishing how you got each thing is not a compliance burden. It is why people
believe the rest of it.

## Consumer pitch

You already trust a handful of people more than any review site. The problem is
that what they actually use is scattered across expired stories, old captions
and comment replies.

A Zat is one link that holds it properly: what they own, how long they have had
it, how often they reach for it, and what they would tell you not to like. With
the commercial relationship stated on every single item, so you can weigh it
yourself.
