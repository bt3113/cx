# Press

## Boilerplate

Zat is a creator-owned personal world: one link holding a person's identity,
taste, tools, work and recommendations. Where a link list shows where someone
exists and a storefront shows what they want you to buy, Zat shows the person —
with structured context on every recommendation, including how it was obtained,
how long it has been used and what the creator dislikes about it. Zat is in
early development.

## FAQ

**What is Zat, in one sentence?**
A personal world you own — one link holding your identity, taste, work and
recommendations, with the context that makes them worth trusting.

**How is it different from a link-in-bio tool?**
A link list points elsewhere. A Zat is the destination. It has structure —
Spaces, Items, disclosures, connected content — so a visitor can actually
explore a person rather than being bounced to five other sites.

**How is it different from an affiliate storefront?**
Order of priority. Storefronts lead with products and treat the person as
packaging. Zat leads with the person and treats commerce as the fourth thing
that matters, after identity, exploration and trust. Affiliate links are
supported, labelled, and never the headline.

**Why is disclosure mandatory?**
Because a recommendation you cannot weigh is worthless. Making the commercial
relationship structural — not a footnote — means a reader knows what they are
reading, and a creator who declares openly gets more credit, not less.

**Does Zat take a cut of affiliate revenue?**
No. Revenue comes from subscriptions. Creator affiliate relationships are
theirs.

**Who is it for?**
Initially fashion, beauty, interiors, photography, travel and tech. In
principle, anyone with strong taste or a serious interest — designers,
developers, chefs, founders, musicians, athletes.

**What is the long-term idea?**
Structured relationships between people, Spaces, Items, brands and content form
a taste graph: a way to ask what the people you respect genuinely use, rather
than what is being advertised hardest.

**What is actually built today?**
A complete working product: profiles, Spaces, Items, the trust layer, content
relationships, Story sharing, saving, discovery, a creator Studio with
publishing, analytics and a media kit. Payments, affiliate networks and social
imports are architected but need third-party credentials, and the product says
so rather than faking them.
