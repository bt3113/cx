# Product principles and demo notes

## Principles

1. **Identity first, exploration second, trust third, commerce fourth.**
   Every design argument resolves against this order. If a change makes
   commerce louder at the expense of identity, it is the wrong change.

2. **A person's world is the product.** Not their catalogue, not their links.
   The measure of success is a visitor thinking "I understand this person
   better now."

3. **Context is not optional.** A recommendation without disclosure, duration
   and drawbacks is an advert. Zat refuses to store one.

4. **Dislikes are a feature.** Listing what is wrong with something is the
   single strongest credibility signal a creator has. The design gives it the
   same weight as praise.

5. **Constrain to protect quality.** Real personalisation within a system that
   cannot produce an ugly result. No free-form page builder.

6. **Say what is not built.** Simulated integrations are labelled as such,
   everywhere, without being asked.

## Demo talking points

- **Open a profile.** The person is the anchor; Spaces orbit them. Nothing on
  screen is selling anything yet — that is the point.
- **Open a Space.** It expands in place, and it is also a real deep link that
  can be shared and prerendered.
- **Open an Item.** Disclosure sits beside the title. Duration, frequency,
  would-buy-again, likes, dislikes and alternatives all carry equal weight.
  Commerce is one quiet link at the bottom, labelled "Where I got it".
- **Show "Seen in".** The Item lists the content it appears in; the content
  lists everything in it. One authored field, both directions.
- **Press Share.** A 1080 × 1920 image is generated in the browser, with the
  disclosure rendered into it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **Open the Studio.** Draft versus published is explicit. Reorder Spaces by
  dragging, or with the keyboard. Edit and publish, then reload the public
  profile to see it change.
- **Build a character.** Generated from an open-source avatar library so a
  creator without a photoshoot still has an anchor for their world.
- **Show analytics.** Per-Space exploration rate, saves, outbound clicks and
  attributed revenue — not one vanity number. Labelled as demo data.

## What to be careful about in a demo

- Say plainly that authentication, persistence and analytics are demo-tier:
  everything lives in the browser, and a real deployment needs a backend.
- Do not imply platform-scale discovery. Search covers a seeded catalogue of
  four creators, and the product says so on the page.
- The creators are fictional.
