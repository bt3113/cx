# Claims and limitations

## Accurate claim

CLEAR-X is a research prototype for verified semantic diff of supported contract clauses. It maps supported clauses into typed canonical legal frames, compares material fields, and returns `EQUAL`, `DIFFERENT`, or `REJECT`.

## What the prototype demonstrates

- Deterministic canonical framing for supported clause patterns.
- Structured field-level legal diffs.
- Rejection-first behavior for unsupported or ambiguous clauses.
- Schema-validated outputs.
- Generated benchmark and verification reports.

## What it does not claim

CLEAR-X does not claim to:

- understand all contract language;
- replace lawyers;
- replace CLM systems;
- provide legal advice;
- guarantee legal equivalence under all jurisdictions;
- outperform commercial contract AI platforms overall;
- parse every real-world commercial contract clause.

## Current evidence level

The current package is best treated as:

- a research prototype;
- a technical artifact;
- a semantic-redline concept;
- a candidate audit layer for contract AI workflows.

The current benchmark is synthetic. Stronger claims require attorney-reviewed real clause pairs and comparison against real LLM, retrieval, and contract-review baselines.

## Recommended external wording

Use:

> CLEAR-X explores verified semantic diff for contract clauses under a declared ontology and rejection-first policy.

Avoid:

> CLEAR-X solves legal AI.

> CLEAR-X replaces contract review.

> CLEAR-X understands arbitrary contracts.
